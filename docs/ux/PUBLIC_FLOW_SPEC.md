# Public flow specification

1. Landing explains the wellness purpose and links to How it works, Pricing,
   Offer, Privacy, and Sign up.
2. Pricing compares Free and Plus without outcome guarantees and shows the
   purchase/cancellation terms before payment.
3. Sign up offers email, VK, and Telegram; SMS is not supported.
4. Before account activation, the user reviews versioned offer/privacy text and
   records required consent separately from optional communications, tracking,
   and AI consent.
5. Success routes to screening; errors retain entered non-sensitive fields and
   provide a recoverable next action.
